ABOUT BIDE HUB

Your Event. Our Network.

Bide Hub is an event-services marketplace connecting people planning events with professional event service providers in Uganda.

Planning an event can mean searching through WhatsApp groups, Instagram, Facebook, referrals and multiple contacts just to find the right service provider. Bide Hub brings event professionals together in one place.

What can you discover on Bide Hub?

  • Sound & event production companies
  • Lighting and LED screen providers
  • Event decorators
  • Photographers
  • Videographers
  • Event venues
  • Other event-related professionals

How Bide Hub works

01. Discover

Find event professionals according to the service you need.

02. Evaluate

Review their services, location, portfolio, ratings and other available information.

03. Contact

Contact the provider directly through their available phone, WhatsApp, website or social media channels.

For event professionals

Bide Hub gives event service providers a professional online profile where potential clients can discover their business, services, portfolio and contact information.

Our vision

Bide Hub is building a strong network of event professionals across Uganda and eventually across East Africa.
